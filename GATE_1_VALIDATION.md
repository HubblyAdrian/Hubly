# Gate 1 — User validation log

Highest-value Gate 1 work: watch real people use `/demo`.  
Do not help. Do not explain. Observe. Then fix the biggest patterns.

**Close rule:** 3 of 5 would actually use this for their business → Gate 1 may close.

---

## Protocol

1. Participant gets only: the `/demo` URL (and maybe “try this — we’re testing a product”).
2. Facilitator stays quiet. No tips, no “click there,” no narrating Hubly.
3. Note where they hesitate, misclick, or look confused (timestamp + beat).
4. When they stop (or finish Enter Hubly / Home), ask **only** the five questions below.
5. After all five sessions, cluster answers → ship fixes for the top 1–3 patterns only.

### Not blockers

- Logo is initials.
- Minor visual polish.

### Is the bar

- Website feels professional enough to publish.
- They’d let customers book.
- They understand what Hubly does without a tour.
