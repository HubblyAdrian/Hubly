# Business Vision

**Status:** 🔒 **Canonical Builder object (Rule #27) — Locked**  
**Complements:** Business Profile · Owner Profile · Business DNA · Research Profile  
**Does not replace:** facts (profiles) or market findings (research)  
**Related:** [HUBLY_MEMORY.md](../HUBLY_MEMORY.md) · [builder/README.md](./README.md) · [CREATIVE_DIRECTOR_ARCHITECTURE.md](./CREATIVE_DIRECTOR_ARCHITECTURE.md)

---

## Why this exists

Hubly Session → Business Profile → Owner Profile → Business DNA → Research Profile answers:

> What is this business **today**, and what does the market look like?

**Business Vision** answers:

> What does the owner want this business to **become**?

That is not business data.  
That is not research.  
It is the **destination**.

Module 4 (AI Creative Director) must build **toward tomorrow’s business**, not only mirror today’s facts.

---

```

  ↓
```

**No Builder module may bypass or duplicate** Business Profile · Owner Profile · Business DNA · Research Profile · Business Vision.

## What Business Vision holds

| Field | Examples |
|-------|----------|
| **Long-term goals** | Hit $250k/year · hire employees next year · open a shop |
| **Ideal customers** | Only luxury vehicles · residential homeowners · commercial contracts |
| **Desired positioning** | The premium detailer in Dallas · the most trusted cleaner in Austin |
| **Growth timeline** | 90-day · 1-year · 3-year milestones |
| **Success metrics** | Recurring revenue % · booking rate · review score · profit margin |
| **Expansion plans** | Second truck · shop location · new service line · franchise later |

### Example statements (destination language)

- “I want to be the premium detailer in Dallas.”
- “I want to eventually open a shop.”
- “I only want to work with luxury vehicles.”
- “I want to hire employees next year.”
- “I want to hit $250k/year.”

## How it differs

| Object | Answers |
|--------|---------|
| Business Profile | What do we sell / where / how is it branded today? |
| Owner Profile | How does this founder prefer to operate? |
| Business DNA | Combined current truth |
| Research Profile | What does the market / competition show? |
| **Business Vision** | **What are we building toward?** |

### Creative Director implication

If Vision says *premium luxury in Dallas*, do **not** generate:

- Budget branding · discount messaging · economy pricing

Generate instead:

- Premium brand · elegant typography · high-end copy · luxury photography · ceramic coating featured first · concierge booking

Vision + Research + DNA together prevent “average shop” output for an ambitious founder.

## Lifecycle

| Event | Behavior |
|-------|----------|
| Seeded | Discovery / Owner Profile goals may propose Vision drafts |
| Refined | Explicit Vision questions or Creative Director confirmation |
| Consumed | Module 4 Creative Blueprint must cite Vision for brand / pricing / journey choices |
| Upgraded | Flows into Permanent Memory with the business (never expires while business exists) |
| Edited | Owner can revise Vision later — Creative Director may offer regenerate with “Ask Why” |

Vision may start partial. Creative Director must not invent a Vision silently — if missing, ask or infer with low confidence and flag.

## Must not

- Collapse Vision into Owner Profile “goals” only (goals are inputs; Vision is the articulated destination)  
- Replace Research with wishful thinking  
- Let Module 4 ignore Vision when present  
- Duplicate Vision fields inside ad-hoc module state  
- Modify this model in UI without reopening architecture
