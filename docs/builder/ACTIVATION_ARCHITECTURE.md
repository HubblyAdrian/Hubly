## Purpose

Turn everything the Agency built into a **real, operational business**.

By completion the owner can:

Accept bookings · Take payments · Sync calendar · Receive leads · Publish website · Run the business

This is **not** where Hubly designs.  
This is where Hubly **connects**.

---

```
🎉 Your business is ready.
Now let's activate it.
I'll help connect everything so customers can start booking immediately.

```

| Designed (complete) | Remaining (activate) |
|---------------------|----------------------|
| ✓ Brand · Website · Booking · Services · Pricing · Marketing | ○ Payments · Calendar · Domain · Hours · Notifications · Launch |

User understands: **designing** the business ≠ **activating** the business.

---

## Experience principle — mission control, not integrations checklist

Do **not** feel like “Connect Stripe / Connect Calendar / Enable notifications.”

Speak like preparing for launch:

| Instead of | Say |
|------------|-----|
| Connect Stripe | **Fuel your business with online payments.** |
| Connect Google Calendar | **Protect your schedule from double bookings.** |
| Enable notifications | **Stay informed the moment customers need you.** |
| Set business hours | **Tell customers when you're ready for them.** |
| Connect domain | **Put your business on its own address.** |

Every step answers **why it matters**, not only what it does. Keeps Reveal momentum into Launch.

## Activation sections

### 1. Payments
Fuel online payments — cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, deposits, taxes, invoices.  
**Connect Stripe** · Skip · Learn More. AI explains why.

### 2. Calendar
Protect from double-booking — Google · Apple · Outlook.  
Skip → Hubly calendar still works; AI reassures.

### 3. Business Hours
AI-suggested hours from DNA / market — review · Edit · Accept · Copy Weekdays.

### 4. Travel Area
If mobile: radius · max distance · travel fee · ZIP / map. If shop: skip automatically.

### 5. Domain
Connect existing or suggest names (`ShineDetailing.com` …). Connect · Buy · Skip.

### 6. Notifications
Email · SMS · Push — booking / review / lead / marketing alerts. Enable All · Customize · Skip.

### 7. Team
Solo → skip. Else invite Manager · Technician · Office · Admin.

### 8. AI Automations
Would you like Hubly to automatically: review requests · abandoned booking recovery · lead follow-up · reminders · membership upsells. Enable All · Choose · Skip.

### 9. Final Activation Review + Launch
Readiness score (e.g. Business Ready 97%). Missing items optional with AI recommendations (e.g. domain → +11% conversion estimate).

**🚀 Launch My Business** — large, centered.

On launch: celebration → “Your business is officially live. Now let's take you to your command center.” → camera-zoom transition into **Home Dashboard** (Operate OS). Builder becomes the Operating System.

---

## Launch rules

**Never block launch** for optional integrations.

Hard minimum only:

- Business Name  
- At least one Service  
- Booking Enabled  

If Stripe / domain / calendar missing: launch allowed + “we'll remind you later.”

---

## Account / Save My Business

First account point remains **Save My Business** (Reveal first edit or final Reveal CTA).  
Activation assumes Session upgraded or soft-account; if somehow still anonymous at Launch, prompt Save My Business before publish — never “Create Account” framing.

## Stage 1 vs Stage 2

| Stage 1 (OS) | Stage 2 |
|--------------|---------|
| Activation checklist UX + readiness score | Live Stripe Connect / Calendar OAuth / Domain purchase |
| Simulated or soft-connect states with honest “connect later” | Full live integrations |
| Launch → Dashboard transition | Filmic zoom / publish pipeline |
| Automations as OS toggles | Vendor-backed automation delivery |

Label Stage 2 clearly — do not claim “Stripe live” until connected.
