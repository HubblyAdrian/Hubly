## Purpose

Answers one question:

> If you were launching this business tomorrow, what would you change first?

Not configuring software. **Coaching.**

Agencies don’t hand over work and vanish. They say: “Before we launch… here’s what I’d recommend.”

---

## Position in pipeline (🔒)

```
Business Activation (Module 6)
  ↓
AI Launch Coach (Module 7)     ← this module
  ↓
🚀 Launch My Business
  ↓
Home Dashboard (Operate OS)
```

**Creative Review** scored what Hubly *built*.  
**Launch Coach** scores whether the business is *ready to operate* — and advises final improvements.

## Layout

- Title: 🎓 AI Launch Coach  
- Business Readiness % + bar  
- “Everything required is complete.”  
- Prioritized recommendation cards  
- Launch checklist (business readiness, not tech settings)  
- Business Health score  
- 90-day forecast (labeled estimates)  
- Growth timeline (Month 1–6)  
- Ask the Coach chat  
- Controls: Apply All · Review One by One · Launch Anyway · Save Recommendations  

---

## Readiness vs Creative Review

| Creative Review (Rule #28) | Launch Coach |
|----------------------------|--------------|
| Brand consistency of the blueprint | Operate readiness (payments, booking, marketing, trust…) |
| Design quality | Launch / go-live quality |
| Before Reveal | After Activation, before Launch |

Categories example: Brand · Website · Booking · Payments · Marketing · SEO · Trust · Growth

---

## Recommendation cards

Prioritized: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW  

Each card: Priority · Impact · Time to complete · AI explanation · Apply · Keep Mine / Mark Later · Why?

Examples:

- Launch with 4 services instead of 6 (conversion)  
- Add before-and-after photos (+14% trust estimate)  
- Raise ceramic pricing toward market  
- Enable memberships ($1,400/mo recurring estimate)  
- Connect Google Reviews (+9% conversion estimate)

**Apply All** · **Review One by One** · **Launch Anyway**

---

## Business Health + Forecast + Timeline

- **Business Health** score with plain-language band (e.g. Excellent)  
- **Next 90 Days** estimates: bookings · revenue · membership potential · reviews — clearly labeled estimates  
- **Growth Timeline:** Month 1 Reviews → Month 2 Memberships → Month 3 pricing → … editable  

All cite DNA / Vision / Research / Activation state when relevant.

---

## Ask the Coach

Persistent chat using full Agency memory:

- Why raise pricing?  
- Should I offer ceramic?  
- Is this website good enough?  
- Would you launch this?

---

## Launch rules

**Never block launch** for optional recommendations.

Hard minimum only (same as Activation):

- Business name  
- ≥ 1 service  
- Booking enabled  

Optional gaps → Recommend, never require.
