## Summary

Inbox Stage 1 ships a complete in-Hubly messaging OS: unified conversations, channel tabs, thread tools, customer sidebar, search/filters, and AI assists. No live Twilio / Meta / Resend / realtime connections are claimed.

## Stage 1 (shipped)

- Unified Inbox page (list · thread · customer sidebar)
- Tabs: All, Website Chat, SMS, Email, Facebook, Instagram, AI, Needs Attention, Archived
- Reply, templates, attachments, images, voice notes, internal notes
- Channel tools (AI/human takeover, booking, quote, schedule, AI rewrite/draft)
- Customer matching, lifetime revenue, membership, vehicles, quick actions
- AI: summary, reply, intent, sentiment, lead detection, suggested actions

## Stage 2 (deferred — separate PR later)

- Twilio live send/sync
- Meta OAuth + Messenger/IG sync
- Resend per-business inbox send
- Realtime websocket sync

## Lock

**Inbox Operating System is locked.**  
Do not modify Inbox OS unless bug fix, Stage 2 integrations, or explicit reopen.
