# Delight Sprint — Polish, Not Features

**Goal:** Five minutes in Hubly should feel like the smoothest software they’ve ever used — not “a lot of AI.”

**Permanent principle:**

> **The output is always more important than the process.**

The customer cares that:

- their website looks amazing  
- their booking page works  
- their CRM is ready  
- they can start getting customers  

Not that Hubly reasoned for 14 seconds.

---

## Rules

1. **No new concepts this sprint** — polish what exists  
2. **Website always visible** in Create (hero)  
3. **One hero per screen**  
4. **Every AI statement needs proof** on screen  
5. **Animate progress, not decoration**  
6. **Celebrate outcomes** (logo, live site, packages) — never architecture  
7. **Silent video test** — understand without reading paragraphs  
8. **Never stare at a spinner** — something meaningful is always changing
